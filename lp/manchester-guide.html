Chapter 3

How co owning works.

Same front door. Same keys. Your name on part of it from day one.

  1. You choose a homePick a home in Manchester that fits your budget, or one you've already found.
  2. You buy your first shareYour home deposit buys a share of the home. You own that share from day one.
  3. You pay rent on the restOnly on the part you don't own yet. Never on your own share.
  4. You buy more over timeEach share you add means less rent, until the home is all yours.

No loan, no interest, and certified Shariah compliant (certificate 2578875-01).
