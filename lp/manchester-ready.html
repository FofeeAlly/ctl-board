Your questions.

Is this a mortgage?

No. You buy a share of your home with your home deposit, pay rent only on the part you don't own yet, and buy more over time. There's no loan and no interest.

What do I need to start?

Your home deposit, your ID, and the Bayuti app. Your Bayuti Score in the app shows your home budget in a couple of minutes.

Is there a credit check?

Not to start. Your Bayuti Score uses the answers you give us. Checks only come later, when you apply for a specific home.

How quickly will I hear back?

Once you submit a home in the app, you'll usually hear back within 24 hours.

Is it Shariah compliant?

Yes. CrowdtoLive is certified Shariah compliant, certificate 2578875-01.
