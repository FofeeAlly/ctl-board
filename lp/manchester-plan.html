Is the guide really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. Just tell us where to send it.

I have no home deposit at all. Is it still for me?

Yes. Chapter four is all about building a plan from where you are today, with a target and a date.

Will you check my credit?

No. Reading the guide and doing your Bayuti Score involve no credit check.

Is CrowdtoLive a mortgage?

No. You buy a share of your home with your home deposit, pay rent only on the part you don't own yet, and buy more over time. No loan and no interest.
